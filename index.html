backup

A simple app that backs up your data to your own Google Drive.

About the App

backup lets you copy the data you choose from your device to your personal Google Drive, so you always have a safe copy that you control. You pick what to back up, and you can restore or delete your backups at any time.

The app is free of ads and does not run its own servers: your data goes straight from your device to your Google Drive.

How We Use Google Services

backup uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API for one purpose only: to upload backups of your data to your Google Drive, and to list, download and delete those backups when you ask.

  • You sign in with your own Google account and approve the Google Drive permission on Google's consent screen.
  • The app accesses only the files it creates or that you select for backup.
  • Your Google data is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See our Privacy Policy for details.
