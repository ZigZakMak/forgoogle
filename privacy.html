Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how backup ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use our application. backup is an app that backs up your data to your own Google Drive.

1. Information We Access and Collect

backup does not collect, store or receive your personal data on any server of ours. We have no servers that hold user data. When you sign in with your Google account and grant permission, the app accesses the following, only on your device and only to provide the backup feature:

  • Google account sign-in: to authenticate you with Google and obtain permission to use Google Drive on your behalf. The sign-in token is kept on your device.
  • Google Drive access: to upload the data you choose to back up, and to list, download and delete the backups created by the app.
  • The data you choose to back up: read from your device and sent directly to your own Google Drive.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing backup and restore features you request. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles or to train any AI or machine learning model.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

backup's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information or Google user data with third parties. Your data travels directly between your device and Google Drive over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Humans do not read your data, except where you explicitly ask us for support and share it, where required for security or to comply with the law.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not store your data, there is nothing to retain on our side. Backups live in your Google Drive and remain there until you delete them, either inside the app or directly in Google Drive. To stop the app from accessing your Google account, revoke its access in your Google Account permissions; uninstalling the app also removes the data it holds locally on your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
